Position / 001 Why this infrastructure

Maritime distress rarely produces a complete evidentiary record.

Distress evidence arrives as partial coordinates, delayed vessel tracks, changing weather, public testimony, messages and institutional reports. Each source has a different clock, resolution, bias and risk.

SeaCommons studies how these heterogeneous fragments can be related to one another without converting uncertainty into unwarranted certainty, and without compromising the safety of the people they describe.

Research question How can heterogeneous maritime signals support timely action while remaining explainable, correctable and safe?

Method / 004 Proof before presentation

Explainability is a methodological requirement.

Analytical authority does not come from visual polish. A useful result must expose what entered the system, what changed, what remains unknown and who can contest it.

01

Provenance

Where the observation came from, when it arrived and how it was obtained.

02

Transformation

Software version, environmental source, parameters and computational path.

03

Uncertainty

Confidence, missing fields, disagreement and sensitivity to assumptions.

04

Contestability

Corrections, alternative interpretations and the record of human decisions.

Lab / 005Next research surface

Browser-based spatial simulation of drift scenarios.

Play runs on CesiumJS in the browser today, using the same scenario data as the operational map. Unreal Engine is planned as an optional high-fidelity renderer for an accredited immersive view, delivered through WebRTC pixel streaming rather than a local install. In both cases the simulation API remains authoritative — the renderer is an instrument for inspection and communication, not a second source of environmental truth.

Operational boundary

This is research infrastructure.
It is not an emergency service.

SeaCommons outputs are experimental and may be incomplete, delayed or wrong. They must not replace official search-and-rescue coordination, emergency communications or qualified operational judgment.

If a life may be at risk, contact the appropriate emergency and maritime rescue authorities through established channels.
